Reads recorded candles for the selected symbol and timeframe and computes RSI for every one. Whenever RSI rises above the overbought level, the next window is predicted to resolve Down; below the oversold level, the next window is predicted to resolve Up. Each window is an independent decision — there is no retry ladder, a miss is simply a loss. Win rate is measured over fully-decided windows, excluding a still-open trailing signal at the very end of the data.
